# Catalogue references

BookNGo uses a sample programme. Prices and booking dates are demonstration values. TBC means the runtime or Malaysian age rating has not been confirmed for this draft.

Street Fighter, Other Mommy, Juminten Edan, Whalefall and Khong Khaek 2 now use the user-supplied poster files in `image/`. Their filenames are matched to the corresponding movie and their URLs are encoded to preserve spaces. These five posters load locally without an external image provider.
